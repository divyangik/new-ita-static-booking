01 / The journey

Colonial charm, Caribbean rhythm.

Immerse yourself in the culture of Cartagena, Colombia’s Caribbean port city.

Cartagena’s 16th-century walled Old Town is a maze of colonial streets, plazas and balconies, framed by beaches and an easygoing local culture. Explore the walled city with a local historian, spend a day on a Caribbean island, dance through a night of music and salsa, and leave time to enjoy the rooftop and pool at Hotel Movich in the heart of the historic center.

02 / Day by day

Five days, to Cartagena’s rhythm.

History, island time and long Caribbean evenings.

01Centro Histórico02Ciudad Amurallada03Island Life04Lifestyle05Departure
  1. Day 01

    Cartagena

    Centro Histórico

    • Our local team welcomes you at the airport and takes you by private shuttle to Hotel Movich.
    • Toast the sunset with a poolside cocktail.
    • Gather for a Welcome Dinner of local dishes at one of the city’s popular restaurants.
    Welcome cocktailWelcome dinner
  2. Day 02

    Cartagena

    Ciudad Amurallada

    • After breakfast, explore the walled Old City on a guided walking tour with a local historian.
    • Pause for coffee in a park, with live local musicians, shopping and dancing.
    • Enjoy dinner, then the evening at your leisure.
    Breakfast
  3. Day 03

    Cartagena · Caribbean island

    Island life

    • After breakfast, take a boat from the marina to a nearby Caribbean island.
    • Enjoy palm trees, white sand, fresh seafood and fresh fruit juices.
    • Return to the hotel in the early evening, then head out for dinner and a night of music and salsa.
    BreakfastMusic & salsa night
  4. Day 04

    Cartagena

    Lifestyle

    • After breakfast, enjoy free time on the rooftop, by the pool or simply relaxing.
    • Optional: our local team can arrange a massage, spa, guided tours, a beach day, horseback riding, fishing, ATV, paddleboarding or other personalized experiences.
    • This evening, celebrate at a Farewell Dinner with dancing and surprises.
    BreakfastFarewell dinner
  5. Day 05

    Cartagena → Departure

    Until next time
