02 / Day by day

Ten days, one shared story.

A journey built around connection: with Thailand, with nature and with one another.

01Arrival02Temples & River03Flavors & Wellness04Northern Thailand05Elephants & Flavors06Art & Soul of Lanna07To the Andaman Sea08Phi Phi Islands09Phang Nga Bay10Departure
  1. Day 01

    Nov 2 · Bangkok

    Welcome to Thailand

    • Arrive at Bangkok Suvarnabhumi International Airport, where the ITA Global team welcomes you for a private transfer to Avani+ Riverside Bangkok Hotel.
    • Set along the Chao Phraya River, Avani+ offers contemporary accommodations and sweeping river and skyline views.
    • Enjoy time to settle in and recover from your international flight.
    • This evening, reunite with the group for a Welcome Dinner overlooking Bangkok, where great food, cocktails and panoramic city views begin your Thailand adventure.
    Welcome dinner
  2. Day 02

    Nov 3 · Bangkok

    Temples, palaces & the River of Kings

    • Visit the magnificent Grand Palace and the revered Temple of the Emerald Buddha, Wat Phra Kaew, followed by the iconic Wat Arun, Temple of Dawn.
    • Experience another side of Bangkok aboard a traditional long-tail boat through the city’s historic klongs, passing riverside communities, temples and everyday life along the waterways.
    • Enjoy a lunch of authentic Thai cuisine before returning to the hotel.
    • The evening is at leisure for Bangkok’s restaurants, rooftop bars and nightlife.
    BreakfastLunch
  3. Day 03

    Nov 4 · Bangkok

    Flavors, art & wellness

    • Begin with a relaxed exploration of the city’s creative and artistic side.
    • Join a guided Old Town food experience, sampling local dishes, street-food favorites and authentic Thai flavors.
    • After lunch, slow the pace with an included traditional Thai massage.
    • The rest of the afternoon and evening is yours to explore ICONSIAM, shop, enjoy the riverfront or simply relax together.
    BreakfastLunch
  4. Day 04

    Nov 5 · Bangkok → Chiang Mai

    Journey to Northern Thailand

    • After breakfast, transfer to the airport for your included flight to Chiang Mai, the cultural heart of Northern Thailand.
    • Transfer to Na Nirand Romantic Boutique Resort, an intimate riverside retreat inspired by the architecture and traditions of the north.
    • Later, visit the legendary Doi Suthep Temple, set high above Chiang Mai with panoramic views across the valley.
    • Continue into the historic Old City, then enjoy the energy, flavors and artisan stalls of the Night Bazaar.
    BreakfastFlight included
  5. Day 05

    Nov 6 · Chiang Mai

    Elephants & the flavors of Thailand

    • Visit an ethical elephant sanctuary dedicated to responsible interaction and the well-being of Thailand’s elephants.
    • Spend meaningful time observing, feeding and walking alongside these gentle giants, with no elephant riding.
    • Later, join a private Thai cooking experience: discover local ingredients, learn traditional techniques and prepare regional dishes together before sitting down to enjoy them.
    BreakfastLunch
  6. Day 06

    Nov 7 · Chiang Mai

    The art & soul of Lanna

    • Visit the artisan communities of San Kamphaeng and Bo Sang, known for silk weaving, hand-painted umbrellas, woodcarving and traditional craftsmanship.
    • Continue deeper into Lanna heritage with a visit to a peaceful temple and an introduction to Buddhist meditation and mindfulness.
    • Return to Chiang Mai with time to relax before an evening at leisure among the city’s restaurants, live music and nightlife.
    Breakfast
  7. Day 07

    Nov 8 · Chiang Mai → Phuket

    From the mountains to the Andaman Sea

    • After breakfast, transfer to the airport for your included flight south to Phuket.
    • Continue to The Shore at Katathani, an adults-only beachfront resort overlooking Kata Noi Beach, and settle into your private pool villa.
    • Board a private sunset catamaran exclusively for the group and sail into the Andaman Sea with music, light bites, cocktails and tropical views as the sun sets.
    BreakfastFlight included
  8. Day 08

    Nov 9 · Phuket · Phi Phi Islands

    The Phi Phi Islands

    • Set out across the Andaman Sea by premium speedboat toward the Phi Phi Islands, surrounded by limestone cliffs and clear tropical water.
    • Visit Maya Bay, then continue to Bamboo Island for swimming and snorkeling.
    • Enjoy an included island lunch before returning to Phuket in the late afternoon.
    • The evening is yours to relax at the resort, enjoy your private pool villa or explore Phuket together.
    BreakfastLunch
  9. Day 09

    Nov 10 · Phuket · Phang Nga Bay

    Phang Nga Bay & a night to remember

    • Explore Phang Nga Bay, where towering limestone formations rise from emerald-green water.
    • Paddle by canoe through hidden lagoons and sea caves.
    • Return to the resort with time to refresh before a Farewell Dinner at a beautiful destination restaurant, celebrating the friendships and memories made along the way.
    BreakfastFarewell dinner
  10. Day 10

    Nov 11 · Phuket → Departure

    Until we meet again

    • Wake to one final morning overlooking the Andaman Sea.
    • Enjoy breakfast and a leisurely morning by your private pool, on the beach or at the spa.
    • At the appropriate time, transfer privately to Phuket International Airport for your journey home.
    Breakfast
