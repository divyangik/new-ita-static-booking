01 / The journey

Drums, food, and a living heritage.

Discover the Garífuna, a people of West African, Arawak and Carib ancestry whose culture is recognized by UNESCO.

Exiled from St. Vincent by the British, the Garífuna arrived in Belize in the 19th century and kept their language, drumming, dance and storytelling alive. On this journey you’ll hear Punta drumming in Dangriga, the culture capital of Belize, taste dishes such as hudut, a coconut fish stew with mashed plantains, and learn the traditions behind celebrations like Garífuna Settlement Day. Between cultural days, explore the Mayan site of Xunantunich and the Inland Blue Hole, and slow down on the beach in Hopkins.

02 / Day by day

Six days, to the rhythm of the drum.

Culture, history and beach time, all from one base in Hopkins.

01Arrival02Hopkins03Dangriga04Mayan Ruins05Beach Day06Departure
  1. Day 01

    Belize City → Hopkins

    Welcome to Belize

    • The team greets you at Belize International Airport.
    • Drive south along the Hummingbird Highway, with photo stops along the way.
    • Check in to The Lodge at Jaguar Reef, a beachfront hotel in Hopkins, and enjoy a sunset cocktail by the pool.
    Sunset cocktail
  2. Day 02

    Hopkins

    Beach life in Hopkins

    • Enjoy free time at the beach resort: walk the white sand and swim in the Caribbean.
    • In the afternoon, stroll through Hopkins and meet the locals.
    • Spend the evening enjoying the hotel’s amenities and cocktails.
    • Today is the best day to add an optional activity or tour.
    Breakfast
  3. Day 03

    Hopkins · Dangriga

    Dangriga, culture capital of Belize

    • Travel to Dangriga, at the mouth of the North Stann Creek River.
    • Experience Garífuna drumming, Punta music and cultural performances.
    • Enjoy a local lunch and visit the Garífuna Museum.
    • Join a hands-on session in drumming, dancing or preparing Garífuna cuisine.
    BreakfastLunch
  4. Day 04

    Xunantunich · Blue Hole National Park

    Mayan pyramids & the Blue Hole

    • Start with a traditional Belizean breakfast.
    • Explore Xunantunich, the “Stone Woman,” one of Belize’s most impressive Mayan sites.
    • Continue to the Inland Blue Hole in Blue Hole National Park.
    • Return to the hotel to relax with a cocktail.
    BreakfastDinner
  5. Day 05

    Hopkins

    A final beach day

    • Spend the day on the beach and swimming.
    • Take an afternoon walk through Hopkins.
    • End with evening cocktails and ocean views.
    Breakfast
  6. Day 06

    Hopkins → Belize City

    Departure

    • Take a morning walk on the beach, enjoy breakfast and capture final photos.
    • Transfer to Belize City International Airport for your flight home.
    Breakfast
