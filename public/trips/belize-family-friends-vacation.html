01 / The journey

Jungle, culture, island time.

A private, curated Belize vacation for families and small groups of friends.

Belize is home to Mayan sites such as Caracol, Lamanai and Altun Ha, deep jungle and a barrier reef dotted with cays. This journey begins on the beach in Hopkins, with cave tubing and ziplining in the jungle and a Garifuna cultural day in Dangriga, then moves offshore to Caye Caulker for island life and snorkeling with sharks and rays at Shark & Ray Alley.

02 / Day by day

Eight days, from jungle to reef.

Four days on the Hopkins coast, then three on Caye Caulker.

01Welcome to Belize02Hopkins03Cave & Zipline04Dangriga05Caye Caulker06Shark & Ray Alley07Relax08Departure
  1. Day 01

    Belize City → Hopkins

    Welcome to Belize

    • Meet your driver-guide at Belize International Airport.
    • Drive south along the scenic Hummingbird Highway, with stops for photos along the way.
    • Check in to Jaguar Reef, a beachfront hotel in Hopkins, and toast the sunset with a poolside cocktail.
    DinnerWelcome cocktail
  2. Day 02

    Hopkins

    Get to know Hopkins

    • Relax on the beach and swim in the Caribbean Sea.
    • In the afternoon, stroll through Hopkins and meet the locals.
    • Spend the evening enjoying the hotel’s amenities.
    BreakfastLunchDinner
  3. Day 03

    Hopkins · Jungle

    Cave tubing & zipline

    • Head into the jungle for guided cave tubing, floating past stalagmites, stalactites and a small waterfall inside an open cavern.
    • Then fly over the treetops and river on a zipline.
    BreakfastLunchDinner
  4. Day 04

    Hopkins · Dangriga

    Dangriga cultural experience

    • Visit Dangriga, the heart of Garifuna culture and Punta music.
    • Enjoy lunch and a visit to the museum.
    • Join a hands-on Garifuna cultural activity.
    BreakfastLunchDinner
  5. Day 05

    Hopkins → Caye Caulker

    Caye Caulker, a day of leisure

    • Transfer to Caye Caulker and check in to Iguana Reef.
    • Spend a free day exploring the island’s shops and bars, or soaking up the sun by the channel.
    • Optional: a sunset cruise, at an additional cost.
    Breakfast
  6. Day 06

    Caye Caulker · Barrier Reef

    Snorkeling at Shark & Ray Alley

    • Snorkel at Shark & Ray Alley, where you can hand-feed sharks and rays.
    • Look out for manatees and colorful reef fish along the way.
    • Fresh fruit and drinks are provided on board before you return to Caye Caulker for the evening.
    BreakfastLunch
  7. Day 07

    Caye Caulker

    Your day to relax

    • A free day to enjoy the island at your own pace.
    • Choose from more than 25 restaurants on Caye Caulker, then end the night dancing.
    Breakfast
  8. Day 08

    Caye Caulker → Belize City

    Departure

    • After breakfast, transfer to Belize City for your flight home.
    Breakfast
