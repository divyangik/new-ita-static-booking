01 / The journey

Three capitals, one festive season.

Experience the magic of a European Christmas on a 12-day holiday journey through Budapest, Vienna and Prague.

Cruise the illuminated Danube, soak in Budapest’s thermal baths and watch Tchaikovsky’s The Nutcracker. Step into imperial Vienna with a guided tour of Schönbrunn Palace, coffeehouse traditions and an evening of classical music. Finish in the Golden City of Prague with Gothic cathedrals, Charles Bridge, a one-of-a-kind beer spa and the glow of the Old Town Christmas markets.

02 / Day by day

Twelve days, three Christmas cities.

From the Danube to the Vltava, a festive journey of markets, music and imperial history.

01Welcome to Budapest02Historic Budapest03Szentendre04To Vienna05Imperial Vienna06Schönbrunn07Leisurely Vienna08To Prague09Beauty of Prague10Beer Spa11Grand Finale12Departure
  1. Day 01

    Budapest

    Welcome to Budapest

    • Arrive in Budapest and check in to your boutique hotel.
    • This evening, gather for a Welcome Dinner, then board a night sightseeing cruise on the Danube.
    • Glide past the city’s illuminated historic buildings and the Chain Bridge.
    Welcome dinner
  2. Day 02

    Budapest

    Discover Budapest’s historic charm

    • Spend the morning exploring Budapest’s landmarks and historic streets.
    • In the afternoon, unwind at one of the city’s famous thermal baths, Széchenyi or Gellért.
    • This evening, wander the Christmas markets for Hungarian folk art and festive treats.
    Breakfast
  3. Day 03

    Budapest · Szentendre

    A picturesque getaway

    • Take a short trip to the charming town of Szentendre, known for its galleries, museums and gift shops.
    • Pause for coffee at a historic café.
    • Back in Budapest this evening, enjoy Tchaikovsky’s The Nutcracker ballet.
    BreakfastThe Nutcracker
  4. Day 04

    Budapest → Vienna

    Travel to Vienna

    • Enjoy a scenic drive of about 2.5 hours from Budapest to Vienna.
    • Check in to your hotel.
    • The evening is free to begin exploring the city.
    Breakfast
  5. Day 05

    Vienna

    Experience imperial Vienna

    • Discover Vienna’s royal landmarks on a guided walking tour or a horse-drawn Fiaker carriage ride.
    • Stop for coffee at a historic café.
    • Visit Vienna’s Christmas markets.
    Breakfast
  6. Day 06

    Vienna

    Step into the Austrian Empire

    • Take a guided tour of Schönbrunn Palace, the former summer residence of the Habsburgs.
    • Enjoy a tea break at a traditional coffeehouse.
    • Optional: an elegant dinner with a classical concert at the palace.
    BreakfastOptional palace concert
  7. Day 07

    Vienna

    Leisurely Vienna

    • A free day to explore Vienna’s streets and neighborhoods at your own pace.
    • This evening, attend a classical concert or the opera.
    Breakfast
  8. Day 08

    Vienna → Prague

    On the way to the Golden City

    • Drive about 3.5 hours from Vienna to Prague.
    • Check in to your hotel.
    • The evening is free.
    Breakfast
  9. Day 09

    Prague

    The beauty of Prague

    • Discover Prague’s Gothic cathedrals and Art Nouveau architecture.
    • Walk across Charles Bridge and explore the Jewish Quarter.
    • This evening, visit the Christmas markets and try a traditional chimney cake.
    Breakfast
  10. Day 10

    Prague

    A unique spa experience

    • Relax at a beer spa: a hot beer bath in a wooden barrel, unlimited fresh tap beer and a rest on a hay bed.
    • This evening, celebrate with Christmas dinner at a recommended restaurant, such as Mlynek or Kampa.
    BreakfastChristmas dinner
  11. Day 11

    Prague

    A grand finale in Prague

    • Climb the Old Town Hall Tower for 360° views over the city and its grand Christmas tree.
    • Visit the Living Crib and the astronomical clock in the main square.
    • Celebrate the journey at a Farewell Dinner, followed by a nighttime walk along the illuminated riverside.
    BreakfastFarewell dinner
  12. Day 12

    Prague → Departure

    Farewell to Europe

    • Enjoy a final breakfast in Prague.
    • Prepare for departure and your flight home.
    Breakfast
